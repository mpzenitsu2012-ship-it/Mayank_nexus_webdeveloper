About

I'm Mayank prasad — a creative developer who treats the browser like a studio. I move between design and engineering, shaping ideas into interfaces that feel alive: precise motion, deliberate typography, and interaction that rewards curiosity.

My process starts with a question — what should this feel like to use? — then works backward through code. I care about the details most people scroll past: the easing on a hover, the weight of a headline, the moment a page reveals itself. Every project is a chance to make something that couldn't have come from a template.
